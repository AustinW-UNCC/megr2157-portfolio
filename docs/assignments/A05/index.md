Lessons Learned - 

4. Governing Failure Mode:

Pin diameter changed to almost double the original size when going from the stress analysis to the stiffness analysis. The stress having a FS of 4 was the much more limiting factor in this case. 

5. Error Propagation -

In my case, nothing ended up propagating incorrectly, however, I believe that I would have had a major issue if I had not read the entire instructions clearly as I used the 300 lbf force instead of the 600lbf total force because I knew that my part was symmetrical. Had I not looked at that properly, my entire catalog of calculations would have been wrong. 

6. Assumption Sensitivity -

I assumed that shear deflections were 0 or non-existent. If I were wrong about this, then the total deflection that I calculated for each figure would be slightly wrong. I do not expect that it would be major, however, there would be the possibility that the FS would not be 100% accurate. This is why having a high factor of safety is crucial when designing, because if there are mistakes that don't get caught, then the factor of safety is there to catch those and allow for minor discrepancies.
