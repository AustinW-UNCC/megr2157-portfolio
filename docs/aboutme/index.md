As an engineering student I strive for solutions. As a child, I grew up working on vehicles with my father and I have forever had a passion for anything with a motor my entire life because of it. Mechanical engineering fit right into my path as I got older and is something I chose to pursue for the love of the process rather than just a paycheck. As an engineer, I am driven 
by a desire to see the automotive industry thrive. Whether I am evaluating a current system or working to design the next iteration, I am interested in the mechanics behind the reactions and not just the final product. When I was a much more inexperienced "engineer" I grew up constantly taking things apart and hoping that I remembered how they went back together. Things like Lego's were a little too simple in my mind and I distracted myself with much larger and more complicated structures and machines. I was always good at math and enjoyed looking into the physics of things. This led me directly to mechanical engineering where those main subjects mold into one and they start to explain the why and the how of everyday life. I cannot even drive down the road without thinking about how the world around me works and the fellow engineers behind the curtains of their designs and processes. This all leads to my final statements. As an engineer, I am someone who will not take things for granted or just accept answers for their face value. I am someone who will seek answers and constantly look for a better way to accomplish the same goal. There is no stopping point when it comes to engineering, and I will always be the kind of engineer who looks for the next best solution.

I feel like I know how I should "defend" a decision, however, at this moment in time, I am not confident that I would be able to properly and successfully defend one of those decisions without help from someone who knows more than me.
